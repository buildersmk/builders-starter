# Prompts — things to ask Claude

You change this app by **describing what you want**, not by editing code. Copy any line below into
Claude and refresh the page to see it. Replace the parts in `[brackets]` with your own words.

These are the same prompts shown in the in-app guide (`/guide` → "Make it yours"). The golden rule:
say what you want to *happen*; let Claude work out the *how*.

## Words & branding
- "Change the homepage headline to `[your headline]` and the subtitle to match."
- "Rewrite the pricing page for `[what you sell]` at `[price]` per month."
- "Rename `[old word]` to `[new word]` everywhere a customer can see it."

## Look & feel
- "Make the main colour a warm orange instead of indigo, across the whole app."
- "Use a rounder, friendlier font for headings."
- "Make the marketing page feel calmer — more white space, fewer boxes."

## Pages & features
- "Add a simple Contact page with a name, email and message form that emails me."
- "Add an FAQ section to the homepage with 5 questions."
- "When someone signs up, send them a welcome email."
- "Add a free trial: new accounts get 14 days of Pro before they pay."

## When something breaks
- "This broke when I ran the app — fix it: `[paste the red error here]`."
- "The `[page name]` page looks wrong on my phone — make it fit."

## Setup help
- "Walk me through setting up `[Stripe / Resend / Kit]` for this app, step by step."
- "Help me deploy this so it's live on `[yourdomain.com]`."
